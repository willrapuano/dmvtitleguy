# FederalTitle competitive SEO roadmap — 2026-08-25

## Objective

Grow qualified organic leads for DMVTitleGuy.io by capturing FederalTitle.com demand where DMV Title Guy has stronger local usefulness, functioning tools, or fresher content. Traffic without a plausible path to a title, escrow, settlement, investor, builder, lender, or agent relationship is secondary.

## Adversarial review update — August 26, 2026

An independent technical SEO, SpyFu/content-economics, and entity/compliance panel rejected the original FIRPTA-first execution order. The revised order prioritizes crawl reliability, compliance truth, and transaction-ready conversion value:

1. Complete the identity, canonical, legal-policy, CMS-resilience, and release-verification foundation.
2. Deploy and capture a Google Search Console query-by-page baseline before consolidating existing URLs.
3. Host the seller net-sheet calculation on DMV Title Guy rather than sending high-intent visitors to PalmAgent.
4. Correct the existing FIRPTA URL for buyer-liability and source accuracy, but do not create another FIRPTA URL or treat the national head term as the primary growth bet.
5. Consolidate overlapping fee, policy-comparison, and closing-cost articles only after GSC identifies the URLs Google already rewards.

## Explicit depriorities

- Broad “contingent meaning” terms have high volume but weak connection to title-service leads and are dominated by major portals and banks.
- Branded competitor terms should not become imitation or comparison pages without a truthful, useful customer decision angle.
- Do not create thin city pages merely to increase URL count. Expand only where jurisdiction-specific data, transaction workflow, or conversion value exists.

## Measurement cadence

Every 30 days, record:

1. SpyFu ranking keywords, clicks, page count, new keywords, gained ranks, and gained clicks for DMVTitleGuy.io.
2. Federal lost ranks, lost clicks, and broken top pages.
3. Google Search Console impressions, clicks, average position, and indexed status for each target cluster.
4. Organic quote starts, contact submissions, intake starts, and completed title orders by landing page.

Success is not “more articles.” It is growth in non-branded page-one rankings and organic conversion actions while canonical, sitemap, and entity tests remain green.
