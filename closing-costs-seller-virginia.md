# Seller Closing Costs in Virginia: What to Expect in 2026

Selling a home in Virginia involves several closing costs that sellers should be aware of. Understanding these expenses can help you budget effectively and avoid surprises at closing. Below, we break down the typical seller closing costs in Virginia and provide insights into how you can estimate your total expenses.

## Common Seller Closing Costs in Virginia

1. **Real Estate Agent Commission**: Typically 5-6% of the sale price, split between the buyer's and seller's agents.
2. **Title Insurance**: Protects the buyer and lender from title disputes. Sellers often pay for the owner's title insurance policy.
3. **Transfer Taxes**: Virginia charges a state transfer tax, and some localities may add additional fees.
4. **Attorney Fees**: If you hire an attorney to handle the closing, expect to pay $500-$1,500.
5. **Recording Fees**: Paid to the county to record the deed transfer.
6. **Outstanding Mortgage Balance**: Any remaining mortgage balance will be paid off at closing.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I negotiate closing costs as a seller?**
A: Some costs, like real estate agent commissions, may be negotiable. Discuss options with your agent.

**Q: Are there any seller concessions in Virginia?**
A: Yes, sellers can agree to pay for certain buyer costs, such as inspection fees or repairs.

**Q: How much is the Virginia transfer tax?**
A: The state transfer tax is $1 per $1,000 of the sale price. Localities may add additional fees.

**Q: When do I pay closing costs?**
A: All closing costs are paid at the settlement table when the sale is finalized.
