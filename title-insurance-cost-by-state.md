# Title Insurance Cost by State: A 2026 Comparison

Title insurance costs vary significantly by state due to differences in regulations, property values, and local practices. Below, we compare title insurance costs across the U.S. and explain the factors that influence pricing.

## Title Insurance Cost Breakdown by State

| State | Average Cost (Owner's Policy) | Notes |
|-------|-------------------------------|-------|
| Virginia | $1,200 - $2,500 | Based on home value; regulated rates. |
| Maryland | $1,000 - $2,200 | Competitive market; rates vary by provider. |
| DC | $1,500 - $3,000 | Higher due to urban property values. |
| California | $1,000 - $2,500 | Competitive market; rates vary. |
| Texas | $1,200 - $2,800 | Regulated rates; includes endorsements. |
| Florida | $1,500 - $3,500 | Higher due to property values and risk. |

## Factors Affecting Title Insurance Costs
1. **Home Value**: Higher-value homes typically cost more to insure.
2. **State Regulations**: Some states regulate rates, while others allow competition.
3. **Local Practices**: Urban areas may have higher costs due to complex title histories.
4. **Endorsements**: Additional coverage can increase the premium.

## FAQ

**Q: Is title insurance required in every state?**
A: While not legally required everywhere, lenders typically require a lender's title insurance policy.

**Q: Can I shop around for title insurance?**
A: Yes, in states without regulated rates, comparing providers can save you money.

**Q: What does title insurance cover?**
A: It protects against title defects, liens, or ownership disputes not found during the title search.

**Q: How long does title insurance last?**
A: The owner's policy lasts as long as you or your heirs own the property.
