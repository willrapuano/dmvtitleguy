# Buyer Closing Costs in Maryland: A Complete Guide for 2026

Buying a home in Maryland comes with various closing costs that buyers need to prepare for. These expenses can add up, so it's essential to understand what you'll owe at closing. Below, we outline the typical buyer closing costs in Maryland and how you can estimate your total expenses.

## Common Buyer Closing Costs in Maryland

1. **Loan Origination Fees**: Charged by the lender for processing your mortgage, typically 0.5%-1% of the loan amount.
2. **Title Insurance**: Protects you and the lender from title disputes. Buyers usually pay for the lender's title insurance policy.
3. **Appraisal Fees**: Required by lenders to assess the home's value, usually $300-$500.
4. **Home Inspection Fees**: Optional but recommended, costing $300-$500.
5. **Prepaid Expenses**: Includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and prepaid interest.
6. **Recording Fees**: Paid to the county to record the deed and mortgage.

## FAQ

**Q: Are closing costs negotiable in Maryland?**
A: Some costs, like lender fees, may be negotiable. Shop around for the best rates.

**Q: Can I roll closing costs into my mortgage?**
A: Some loans allow this, but it will increase your loan amount and monthly payments.

**Q: How much is title insurance in Maryland?**
A: Title insurance typically costs 0.5%-1% of the home's purchase price.

**Q: When do I pay closing costs?**
A: All closing costs are paid at the settlement table when the purchase is finalized.
