# Who Pays Closing Costs in DC? A 2026 Breakdown

Closing costs in Washington, DC, are typically split between buyers and sellers, but the specifics can vary based on negotiations and local customs. Below, we clarify who pays what in a DC real estate transaction and how you can estimate your share of the costs.

## Who Pays What in DC?

### **Buyer Responsibilities**
1. **Loan Origination Fees**: Charged by the lender for processing the mortgage.
2. **Title Insurance**: Protects the buyer and lender from title disputes.
3. **Appraisal and Inspection Fees**: Required to assess the home's value and condition.
4. **Prepaid Expenses**: Includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and prepaid interest.

### **Seller Responsibilities**
1. **Real Estate Agent Commissions**: Typically 5-6% of the sale price, split between agents.
2. **Transfer Taxes**: DC charges a 1.1% transfer tax, split between buyer and seller.
3. **Title Insurance for Buyer**: Sellers often pay for the owner's title insurance policy.

## FAQ

**Q: Can closing costs be negotiated in DC?**
A: Yes, buyers and sellers can negotiate who pays certain fees, such as transfer taxes or inspection costs.

**Q: Are there any programs to help with closing costs in DC?**
A: DC offers some assistance programs for first-time homebuyers, including grants for closing costs.

**Q: How much is the DC transfer tax?**
A: The transfer tax is 1.1% of the sale price, typically split between buyer and seller.

**Q: When do I pay closing costs?**
A: All closing costs are paid at the settlement table when the transaction is finalized.
